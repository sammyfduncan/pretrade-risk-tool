# Conservative volatility-risk policy

**Design locked:** 2026-07-31 before generating any intraday-risk forecast  
**Depends on:** `docs/intraday_risk_design.md`  
**Status:** joint forecast gate passed; B0 plus GVZ plus calendar is the
registered selection. The policy remains disabled pending a retained primary
strategy and registered economic test
**Direction:** unchanged; this policy may only reduce or block risk

## 1. Purpose

This policy translates a retained, causal two-hour volatility forecast into a
small set of auditable risk multipliers. It does not use trade labels, strategy
P&L, direction, account recovery logic, or a threshold selected from outcomes.

## 2. Forecast eligible to drive the policy

The price/session B0 forecast must first pass its absolute baseline gate in
`docs/intraday_risk_design.md`. The active forecast is then fixed as follows:

- neither F-IV nor F-CAL passes: B0 only;
- exactly one family passes: B0 plus that family;
- both families pass: B0 only until a separately registered joint model passes;
- B0 fails: no volatility policy is deployable.

This avoids choosing whichever of two families produced the more flattering
development result. A model artifact must state the registered feature set,
development cutoff, source hashes, fold specification, and gate verdict. An
artifact without an explicit passing verdict is rejected.

## 3. Expanding forecast rank

At decision time `t`, rank the finite forecast against forecasts whose decision
times are strictly earlier than `t`. The reference may contain only:

- historical out-of-fold forecasts generated by the registered walk-forward
  procedure; and
- forecasts recorded prospectively after the artifact was activated.

In-sample fitted values are forbidden. At least 500 earlier forecasts are
required. The 80th and 95th percentiles use NumPy's `higher` order statistic so
the thresholds are observed past values and ties resolve conservatively.

## 4. Frozen risk mapping

| Current forecast | Multiplier | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| below prior 80th percentile | `1.00x` | retain existing risk cap |
| at/above prior 80th and below prior 95th | `0.50x` | halve allowed stop risk |
| at/above prior 95th | `0.00x` | block the entry |

The multiplier applies to `RiskLimits.risk_per_trade`, whose version-1 maximum
is `0.25%` of equity. Therefore the three maximum risk budgets are `0.25%`,
`0.125%`, and zero. The policy never increases size.

## 5. Fail-closed conditions and precedence

The result is `0.00x` if any of these holds:

- forecast artifact is absent, unapproved, stale, or schema-incompatible;
- forecast or decision timestamp is non-finite/invalid;
- fewer than 500 valid strictly earlier reference forecasts exist;
- a reference forecast is dated at or after the current decision;
- source freshness, candle-contiguity, scheduled-blackout, current spread,
  daily/weekly loss, position-overlap, entry-count, or kill-switch gate fails.

Existing hard gates take precedence. A `1.00x` volatility result is permission
only to continue through the remaining checks, never permission to override
them. Broker minimum-size and notional caps still apply after the risk budget
is reduced; if minimum size cannot fit, the trade is refused.

Every decision records the current forecast, prior thresholds, reference count,
multiplier, reason code, model/version identifier, and decision timestamp.

## 6. Interpretation and later evaluation

This mapping is frozen before any forecast outcome. It is deliberately coarse
because a smooth sizing formula would introduce parameters that could be tuned.

Implementation and synthetic tests may proceed now. The policy remains disabled
on real trades. It may be evaluated economically only after a primary strategy
is retained, under a matched constant-risk comparison registered before P&L is
read. Passing the volatility-information gate alone does not prove that this
mapping improves returns or drawdown.
