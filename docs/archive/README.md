# Historical research archive

These files preserve completed or retired experiments. They are not active
implementation instructions. They remain because each contains at least one
unique preregistration, result, provenance decision or negative finding needed
to audit how the project reached its current risk-framework scope.

## Directional and alternative-data program

- `step1_freeze_record.md` — original pipeline audit and freeze record.
- `step2_design.md` — COT/GDELT design, validation ledger and negative results.
- `step2b_vol_design.md` — volatility-family preregistration and results.
- `intraday_ml_retraining.md` and `intraday_filter_gate.md` — candidate-only ML
  training and the failed economic filter gate.

## Intraday price and execution experiments

- `intraday_v2_design.md` and `intraday_m15_execution_design.md` — revised
  primary rules and the matched M15 execution challenger.
- `intraday_market_structure_research.md` — LBMA-auction hypothesis.
- `intraday_cost_measurement.md` — cost measurement design and interpretation.

## Volatility-overlay experiments

- `intraday_risk_design.md`, `intraday_joint_risk_design.md`,
  `intraday_risk_policy.md` and `intraday_risk_economic_design.md` — historical
  volatility forecasts and strategy-sizing policy. These are distinct from the
  active standalone pre-trade risk framework.

## Futures and cross-market experiments

- `cme_futures_data.md`, `intraday_futures_design.md` and
  `intraday_order_flow_design.md` — GC futures acquisition, bar features and
  order-flow tests.
- `intraday_macro_impulse_design.md` — ZN/6E macro-impulse test.
- `intraday_relative_value_design.md` — gold/silver relative-value test.

## Chronological ledger

- `intraday_rebuild_plan.md` — detailed build and research chronology. It is
  retained as evidence, not used as the current roadmap.
